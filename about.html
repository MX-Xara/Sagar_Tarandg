About Alibaug

Alibaug is a coastal town in the Raigad district of Maharashtra and is part of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

Located approximately 96 kilometers from Mumbai and 143 kilometers from Pune, Alibaug is known for its peaceful beaches, greenery, and coastal charm.

The region is rich in history and culture. The name Alibaug originates from 'Alichi Bagh', meaning Ali's Garden.

Alibaug's unspoiled beaches, lush landscapes, and proximity to the Western Ghats make it one of Maharashtra's most beloved coastal destinations.

Today it remains a popular retreat for families, business travelers, and weekend visitors seeking relaxation by the sea.
